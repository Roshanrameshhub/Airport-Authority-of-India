1. Executive Summary & Institutional Objective

The AAI Asset Management System is a centralized IT lifecycle governance platform engineered for an Airports Authority of India Regional Office. Prior to this platform, equipment records were maintained in physical paper registers and isolated Excel workbooks, exposing the regional office to custody ambiguities, untracked component swaps, expired AMC contracts, and cumbersome annual audits.

Key Objectives Delivered:

  • Unified Digital Inventory: Eliminates paper register books by tracking all computers, laptops, monitors, UPS units, printers, and network switches in a centralized database.
  • Custody Chain & Handover Slips: Enforces an unbroken custody timeline via atomic Assign, Transfer, and Return workflows with downloadable, printable PDF handover slips.
  • IT Service Desk Ticketing: End-to-end complaint management with auto-generated ticket IDs (TKT-XXXXXXX), priority matrix (P1–P4), and status transitions.
  • Automated Equipment Tagging: Auto-generates structured Asset IDs (AAI-REG-XX-YYYY-NNNN) and 300 DPI QR-code physical stickers.
  • Enterprise Excel Ingestion: Ingestion pipeline capable of parsing, decrypting password-protected sheets, and dynamically mapping non-standard legacy spreadsheets.
  • Physical Verification Campaigns: Annual audit workflow with mobile-friendly QR verification lookups.

4. Master Feature Inventory & Operational Workflows

Asset Lifecycle & Custody Transitions

[Unassigned Asset] ──── (Assign) ────► [Assigned to Staff]
        ▲                                     │
        │                                (Transfer)
    (Return)                                  │
        │                                     ▼
[Godown / Pool] ◄────────────────── [Transferred Staff]
        │
    (Damage) ──► [Under Maintenance / Repair] ──► (Fixed) ──► [Godown]
        │
    (Obsolete) ─► [Retired / Written Off]
  • Asset ID Auto-Generation: Sequential AAI-REG-[CATEGORY]-[YEAR]-[SEQUENCE] IDs generated via atomic counter sequences.
  • Atomic Two-Step Custody: Updating custody on an asset synchronously creates an immutable record in AssetAssignment with timestamp and officer ID.
  • Handover Slips: PDFKit generates official AAI slips with custodian acknowledgement blocks.
  • Helpdesk Triage: Tickets follow OPEN → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED → CLOSED with automatic asset maintenance updates.
  • Annual Physical Verification: Field officers verify equipment by scanning QR tags or searching Asset IDs, marking results as VERIFIED, NOT_FOUND, DAMAGED, or MOVED.

8. Recent Architectural Fixes & UI Hardening

  • Asset Detail Drawer Redesign: Structured custodian card into a 2×2 CSS grid, placed the PDF button on its own row, added overflow-wrap: anywhere to eliminate serial number clipping, and mapped status/warranty badges dynamically.
  • Universal Fetch Interceptor: Patched window.fetch in api.js to ensure relative /api/v1 calls route cleanly to production backends (Render) without breaking local dev proxies.
  • Blob-Based Authenticated PDF Download: Secure client-side blob download pipeline injecting Bearer token headers into binary streams.
